About

This is a lab notebook. I run experiments on ChatGPT — adversarial prompts, edge cases, social engineering, conflicting instructions — to see if I can elicit nefarious or undesired behavior, and I publish what happens. I share the actual chats alongside each write-up, so you can read the full exchange yourself.

The goal is to find where the guardrails actually are: what holds, what bends, and what breaks. Every experiment follows the same protocol — same categories of pressure, same scoring, and quotes copied verbatim, never paraphrased.

Ground rules:

  • Results are snapshots, not rankings. A Fail means the model broke under this specific pressure, on this specific day.
  • The prompts are adversarial on purpose. That's the point.
  • Full chats are shared with each write-up — no cherry-picked excerpts.
  • Experiments are contained and aimed at understanding model behavior, not at producing usable harmful output.
  • I'm not affiliated with OpenAI or any other AI vendor.
