About

This blog is exactly what it sounds like: we stress test AI chatbots and publish what happens.

Every test follows the same protocol — same categories of pressure, same scoring, and quotes copied verbatim, never paraphrased. Model versions and dates are recorded with every result, because a stress test without a version number is just a vibe.

A few ground rules:

  • Results are snapshots, not rankings. A Fail here means "broke under this specific pressure, on this specific day."
  • The prompts are adversarial on purpose. That's the point.
  • We're not affiliated with any AI vendor.
